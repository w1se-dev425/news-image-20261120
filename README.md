Sums up Friday 20 November 2026's world news in one image: a newspaper front page. The lead story is the peace declaration issued by Trump, Xi and Putin a day after their first trilateral in Shenzhen, illustrated with an AI-drawn skyline of the city under the three flags, doves overhead. Also on the page: Anthropic's shares soar on their stock-market debut, Britain pledges 1,000 drones for Ukraine, Trump pardons Pete Rose posthumously, and the COP31 draft drops fossil fuels — plus a briefs strip (Meta rethinks its net-zero goal, a Van Gogh sells for a record $190.4m, Somalia expels the UAE ambassador). The program checks the bundled front page (`entry.png`) and copies it to `/output/entry.png`, so it is deterministic and offline: two runs write identical bytes.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
